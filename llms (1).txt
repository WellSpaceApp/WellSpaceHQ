# WellSpace HQ

> WellSpace HQ is the marketing and information site for WellSpace, a free student wellness web app. This page explains what WellSpace is and links to the actual app.

## What it is
WellSpace HQ is a landing page aimed at teachers, schools, and students, explaining what the WellSpace app does, how privacy works, and how to get started. It is not the app itself.

## The actual app
The WellSpace app (mood check-ins, journal, teacher dashboards) lives at:
https://wellspaceapp.github.io/WellSpace/

## Who WellSpace is for
- High school students who want a simple daily wellness check-in.
- Teachers and schools who want a general sense of classroom wellbeing and early signals when a student may need support.

## Privacy and cookies
- This marketing page uses no accounts and collects no personal or health data.
- It uses privacy-safe Google Analytics (only after visitor consent) for basic visit stats only, nothing personal.
- The main app has its own separate Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy covering account and health data, since that is where that data actually lives.
